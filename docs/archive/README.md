This archive contains historical design, implementation and validation reports.
They describe the revision and scope stated in each report; they are not current
product instructions or a fresh validation of the released Beta.

For current capabilities, use the [documentation index](../README.md).
Third-party legal notices, release manifests and source-correspondence materials
retain their original locations. The [engineering index](../engineering/README.md)
links to operational and provenance records kept at stable paths.
